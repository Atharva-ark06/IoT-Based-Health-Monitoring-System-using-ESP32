# Purpose and Consent Review

Before collecting readings from a real person, explain the collection purpose, intended users, storage location, retention period, and known limitations in clear language.

- Do not imply that the system provides diagnosis, emergency monitoring, or clinical recommendations.
- Obtain any consent or institutional authorization required for the actual use case.
- Make participation and withdrawal behavior clear where applicable.
- Do not reuse readings for a new purpose without a fresh review.
- Keep demonstration data synthetic and visibly labelled.

This academic prototype is not a medical device. This document does not establish legal compliance or replace institutional review.
