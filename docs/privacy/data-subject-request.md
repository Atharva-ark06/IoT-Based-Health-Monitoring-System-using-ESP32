# Data Request Handling

If the prototype is ever used with identifiable data, define a verified request-handling process before launch.

- Authenticate the requester through an approved channel; do not rely on an email address alone.
- Verify scope and authority before disclosing or deleting records.
- Locate data in the primary database, exports, and applicable backups.
- Record the request ID and action metadata without copying sensitive content into the audit trail.
- Escalate legal exceptions or ambiguous identity cases to the designated privacy owner.
- Verify completion and communicate through the approved channel.

Do not promise a response deadline until applicable law and institutional policy have been reviewed.
