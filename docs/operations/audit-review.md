# Audit Review Procedure

Review audit metadata on a defined schedule and after security incidents.

- Look for repeated authentication failures, unexpected device identities, unusual request patterns, and maintenance operations.
- Correlate timestamps and request identifiers without copying health payloads into review notes.
- Confirm event retention and access controls remain appropriate.
- Document findings, investigation references, owner, and remediation.
- Escalate possible compromise through incident triage.

Audit records may be incomplete or process-local. Do not represent them as tamper-proof evidence unless integrity protections have been designed and validated.
