# Potential Data Breach Workflow

When unauthorized access to personal data is suspected, promptly involve responsible institutional, privacy, and legal contacts.

1. Preserve evidence and establish what data and systems may be affected.
2. Determine whether the event is confirmed, suspected, or ruled out.
3. Identify applicable legal, contractual, and institutional notification obligations with qualified counsel.
4. Record decisions and deadlines in a restricted incident log.
5. Coordinate external communication through an authorized owner.
6. Avoid publishing identifying details while facts are being established.

This workflow is not legal advice and does not specify a universal notification deadline.
