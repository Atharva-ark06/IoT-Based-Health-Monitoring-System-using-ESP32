# Incident Severity Guide

Classify incidents by impact and urgency; adapt levels to the actual deployment.

- **SEV-1:** confirmed unauthorized disclosure, active credential compromise, or broad integrity loss.
- **SEV-2:** sustained service outage or credible attack affecting multiple users/devices.
- **SEV-3:** contained defect, isolated device failure, or suspected abuse with limited impact.
- **SEV-4:** low-risk operational issue or documentation gap.

When uncertain, escalate rather than downgrade. Assign an incident lead and keep a timestamped decision log.
